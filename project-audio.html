This creative media technology project showcases the integration of digital audio hardware and interactive sound engineering systems. The primary focus is placed on producing a high-quality audio delivery system to support the multimedia application ecosystem.

One of the key milestones in this module is the development of a custom voice narration using one's own voice recording (native voiceover mastering), which was meticulously edited for educational application branding. Through the process of sound engineering, the generated audio assets were successfully integrated into the system to deliver a clear and immersive surround sound impact.
